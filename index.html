The problem

Weather numbers alone leave the practical question unanswered.

A temperature or air quality reading is a starting point. Exposure can vary by street, time of day and the activity someone is doing. People need that context when they choose a route, schedule a session or plan demanding work outdoors.

Scientific foundation

Why we look at heat and PM2.5 together.

Fine particle pollution is associated with cardiovascular harm. Heat also strains the body, particularly during physical activity. People can encounter both on the same day. Research has established the effects of each exposure, while their combined effect in specific situations is still being studied. We use the available evidence to make both conditions easier to understand in context.

Applications

Different decisions, different products.

GOTime serves outdoor movement. For platforms, work sites, infrastructure and health organisations, FerryWellCo brings the same environmental intelligence into a format suited to each setting.

01

Ground and site operations

Site-level analysis and a practical debrief for teams planning work outdoors, including airport ground handling, construction, logistics and agriculture.

02

Infrastructure and local environments

Local environmental profiles for data centres and industrial sites, using available data and site measurements where the question requires them.

03

Apps, wearables and wellness

Local heat, PM2.5, pollen and wind context inside fitness, mapping, wearable and employee wellbeing services.

04

Insurance and health planning

Environmental exposure analysis for insurers, occupational-health teams and care organisations working on population and operational decisions.
