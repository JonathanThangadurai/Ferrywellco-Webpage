GOTime · Outdoor planning

When should you go for that run?

Morning and evening can bring different heat and fine-particle pollution (PM2.5). Heat adds strain to activity, and you breathe more air when you move. We are developing GOTime to show those conditions beside a planned run, walk or ride, so you can compare times and places.

Why FerryWellCo began

We wanted this research to be useful before people head outside.

FerryWellCo began with research into heat, PM2.5 and cardiovascular health. That work raised a practical question: how could someone use what we know when choosing a time or place to be active? GOTime is our first product concept built around that question.

GOTime

See what changes when the plan changes.

Choose a run, walk, ride or group session, then set a time and place. GOTime is designed to show the estimated heat and PM2.5 outlook for that plan and let you compare another option. Explore an example of the planning brief.

How it works

Start with the activity, then add the conditions.

A place, time and activity give the forecast a purpose. GOTime is being developed to place heat and PM2.5 estimates beside that plan and make alternatives easier to compare, without asking you to interpret separate readings.

Scientific foundation

Our work begins with research on PM2.5, heat and cardiovascular health.

Fine-particle pollution and heat each have well-established effects on health. Their combined effect in a particular situation is still an active research question. We use that evidence to decide which environmental information belongs in an outdoor planning tool.

Beyond GOTime

The research also has uses beyond personal activity.
