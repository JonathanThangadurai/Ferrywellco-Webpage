FerryWellCo · GOTime

Should you head out as planned, or choose another time?

A weather forecast can tell you it will be hot. An air-quality app can show fine-particle levels. If you are planning a run, walk or ride, you still have to work out what those readings mean together for your activity. GOTime helps you compare the conditions around different times and places.

The problem

The readings are available. The decision is still left to you.

Imagine a running club has a session planned for late afternoon. Heat is building, and PM2.5 may change over the day. Should the group keep the time, move it or consider another place? Checking separate forecasts leaves the organiser to piece together the answer. Individuals, clubs and the services they use face the same planning question.

Our first product concept

GOTime puts that comparison in one place.

Start with a planned run. GOTime places estimated heat and PM2.5 alongside its time and location, so people can compare another time or place and see what changed. The same approach can support walks, rides and group sessions.

How it works

We start with the conditions, then look at the activity.

FerryWellCo works with estimates of current and future heat and PM2.5. We place them alongside where and when an activity happens. GOTime turns that comparison into a view people can use while planning, rather than leaving them to interpret several separate readings.

Scientific foundation

Our work begins with research on PM2.5, heat and cardiovascular health.

Fine-particle pollution and heat each have well-established effects on health. Their combined effect in a particular situation is still an active research question. We use that evidence to decide which environmental information belongs in an outdoor planning tool.
