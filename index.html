FerryWellCo · Our work

Heat and polluted air place extra strain on the body during outdoor activity.

We started FerryWellCo to bring what research tells us about heat and air pollution into the choices people make outdoors. We turn environmental research and spatial intelligence into tools that wellness platforms can use at that moment.

Why this matters

What does someone need to know before a run?

Heat makes the body work harder to cool itself and can stress the heart. During exertion, we breathe more air, including any pollution in it. Both conditions can change with time and place. A person planning a run may see a temperature and an air-quality reading, yet still face the practical question: go at 15:20 or consider later?

Our first product concept is GOTime. It lets a wellness app compare conditions for a planned run with another available time, where the plan is made.

Our approach

We connect the conditions to what someone is doing.

FerryWellCo brings together environmental research, spatial data and the context of an activity. We work with wellness platforms to turn that information into something people can use while planning time outdoors.

The research

Why these conditions matter to the body.

Heat can place strain on the heart, while physical activity increases the amount of air a person breathes. Air pollution includes particles and gases; PM2.5 is one example alongside nitrogen dioxide and ground-level ozone. Pollen is another outdoor condition we are considering. Our work starts with this evidence, then asks what a person needs to know for the activity ahead.

The people behind the work

Why we started FerryWellCo.

Our team began with research into air pollution, heat and cardiovascular health. We wanted that knowledge to reach the decisions people make in everyday life. GOTime is our first attempt to bring it into an outdoor plan.
