GOTime · Outdoor planning

When should you go for that run?

Morning and evening can bring very different heat and fine-particle pollution (PM2.5). GOTime puts both beside your planned run, walk or ride, so you can pick the better time and place before you head out.

GOTime

The readings are available. The decision is still left to you.

A running club has a session planned for 18:00. Heat builds through the afternoon, and PM2.5 can shift over the day. Should the group keep the time, move it to the morning, or meet somewhere else?

GOTime shows the heat and PM2.5 outlook for that session beside the alternatives, so the organiser can compare 18:00 with 08:00, or one place with another, in a single view.

How it works

Start with the activity, then add the conditions.

A place, a time and an activity give the forecast a purpose. GOTime places heat and PM2.5 estimates beside that plan and shows how the alternatives compare, so you do not have to interpret separate readings. The KNMI hittekracht index shows how demanding the heat can feel. GOTime adds fine-particle pollution and the activity you have planned.

Scientific foundation

Our work begins with research on PM2.5, heat and cardiovascular health.

Fine-particle pollution and heat each have well-established effects on health. Their combined effect in a particular situation is still an active research question. We use that evidence to decide which environmental information belongs in an outdoor planning tool.
