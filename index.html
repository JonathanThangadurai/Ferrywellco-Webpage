For outdoor activity platforms

Give your users an answer to “When should I go?”

GOTime is a planning layer for outdoor activity apps. It compares heat and air quality for an activity someone has planned with another available time, then explains the difference inside the app they already use.

The problem

A forecast gives you readings. You still have to make the decision.

Someone has a run planned for later today. Their app shows a temperature and an air-quality reading, but leaves them to work out whether another time would fit the plan better. GOTime brings that comparison into the planning moment.

Our approach

Put the conditions beside the plan.

FerryWellCo connects environmental research with the decisions people make before going outdoors. A planned activity gives heat and air-quality information a purpose: it lets someone compare options, rather than interpret several separate readings.

The research

Which conditions belong in an outdoor plan?

Air pollution includes particles and gases; PM2.5 is one example alongside nitrogen dioxide and ground-level ozone. Heat involves more than temperature alone. Our research also considers pollen as a separate outdoor condition. The product starts with the information that is useful for the decision at hand.
