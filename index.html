FerryWellCo · What we are building

Heat and air pollution affect how we live and work

Heat can strain the body, and polluted air can matter more during exertion. FerryWellCo brings air-quality science, heat-stress research and spatial information into services people already use. GOTime is our first prototype for outdoor activity. We are also exploring outdoor work and insurance.

Why this matters

What the numbers leave out

Heat can place strain on the heart. During exertion, a person breathes more air, including pollutants. Pollen can be another consideration. What matters changes with the place, the time and what someone is about to do. A person planning an activity or a team organising outdoor work still needs an answer they can use.

Our first working prototype

What if the app helped with the decision?

GOTime starts with a run someone already has in mind. Inside a fictional activity app, it suggests a next step, shows a time window when one fits the plan, and explains the heat and air-quality conditions behind the answer.

How we work

We want environmental research to reach the people who need it

Our starting point is the relationship between outdoor conditions and the person or group affected by them. We bring research and spatial context into a service they already use. GOTime applies that approach to outdoor activity. We are exploring how the same foundation could support outdoor work and other settings.
