Applications

Environmental context for workers, platforms and facilities.

GOTime is our product concept for outdoor movement. FerryWellCo also applies environmental data to questions faced by workplaces, digital services and facilities. Each setting calls for a different view of the information.

GOTime · outdoor movement

For the person planning to go outside.

GOTime puts the local heat and PM2.5 outlook next to a planned run, walk, ride or group session. People can compare times and places, then see the conditions behind each option.

Individual activity

Make conditions easier to consider when choosing when or where to run, walk or cycle.

Clubs and groups

Give organisers a shared view of the conditions around a planned outdoor session.

Fitness partners

Bring GOTime information into the apps and services people already use to plan activity.

Other applications

The information changes with the decision.

A worker, a site manager and an insurer need different answers. We shape the analysis around the decision each one makes.

Outdoor worker planning

Site-level heat and air-quality nowcasts and forecasts can support a practical analysis and debrief for teams planning demanding work. This is a broader worker duty-of-care question, from construction and maintenance to logistics and agriculture.

Platforms and wearables

Environmental context can sit inside existing wellness, mapping, fitness or wearable services, close to the moment a user plans an activity.

Facilities and local environments

Spatial analysis around a data centre, industrial campus or other facility can help teams understand heat and air quality in its local setting.

Insurance and health planning

Population-level environmental exposure analysis can inform prevention and operational planning for insurers and health organisations.
