Applications

Different settings need different information.

Our work starts with heat and PM2.5 data, then takes shape around the decision at hand. GOTime is our product concept for walking and outdoor sport. For work sites, infrastructure and partner services, we use separate formats suited to those teams.

GOTime · walking and outdoor sport

Plan outdoor activity with the conditions in view.

GOTime puts heat and PM2.5 forecasts next to a planned run, walk, ride or group session. People can compare times and places, then understand the conditions behind the choice.

01

Athletes and outdoor sport

Compare possible training times and places with the heat and PM2.5 outlook visible alongside the session.

02

Walking and everyday movement

Make a walk easier to plan by seeing how the conditions differ between a morning route, an afternoon errand or a group outing.

03

Clubs and organised activity

Help organisers consider the conditions before setting a session and communicate the reasoning to participants.

Ground and site operations

A site analysis for people planning outdoor work.

Airport ground handling, construction, logistics, maintenance and agricultural fieldwork all involve demanding tasks outdoors. Our proposed site offering combines a local heat and PM2.5 outlook with the work schedule in a short analysis and debrief.

The brief focuses on the conditions, task windows and practical options that a site lead can discuss with the team. It is a separate application from GOTime.

Infrastructure and local environments

Environmental profiles around facilities.

For data centres and industrial campuses, we look at heat and PM2.5 conditions around the facility, its layout and its operating context. This creates a clearer picture of the local environment for the people managing the site.

Assessing a facility’s own effect on nearby conditions is a more specific question. That work needs site measurements and evidence about the facility itself.
