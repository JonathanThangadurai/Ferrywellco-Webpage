About FerryWellCo

Why we started FerryWellCo

We began with research into air pollution, heat and cardiovascular health. We kept coming back to a practical question: how does that knowledge reach someone deciding when to go outdoors?

GOTime is our first product concept. The phone example shows how a comparison of outdoor conditions could sit in the app where someone is already planning an activity.
