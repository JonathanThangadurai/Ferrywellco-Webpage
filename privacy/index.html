Privacy

A straightforward note about contact and data

This website offers an email link for conversations with FerryWellCo. It does not have a contact form or a visitor account.

When you email us

If you send an email to research@ferrywellco.com, we receive the address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your enquiry and continue a conversation you request. Our email provider processes messages as part of providing the email service.

Please do not send sensitive personal information about workers through this contact route.

Website use

This website does not set optional analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not ask visitors to enter personal information on the site. The hosting provider processes basic connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the site and maintain security.

Your choices

You can ask what information you have sent us, request a correction or ask us to delete an email conversation by writing to research@ferrywellco.com. We retain limited information where a legal obligation applies.

Updates

We will update this page if we add a form, analytics or a different contact process. Last updated: 17 October 2026.
